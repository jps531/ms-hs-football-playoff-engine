1.	Head-to-head competition in the designated region games between the tied teams: three or more teams this will be combined records of the teams involved in the tie.
2.	How the tied teams did against the highest ranked or seeded teams in the designated
3.	games.
a.	Example: Kentucky 9-3 Arkansas 9-3
b.	Records: Kentucky 9-3 Arkansas 9-3 MS State 8-4 Florida 7-5 Alabama 6-6
c.	The tie would be broken by how Kentucky and Arkansas came out against MS State, then Florida, and then Alabama. Ties for first place should be broken first.
4.	Points differential in ONLY the designated region games involving the tied teams, with a maximum point differential of 12 per game.
a.	For example, if New Albany beat Lumberton by 4, Lumberton beat Winona by 17 (capped at 12), and Winona beat New Albany by 5, New Albany would have -1 points, Lumberton would have 8 points, and Winona would have -7 points. This means Lumberton would be first, New Albany would be second, and Winona would be third.
b.	NOTE: When more than two teams are tied and a team(s) is eliminated from the tiebreaker and two teams remain tied with identical score differences, the tiebreaker will revert to the first step.
i.	For example, if Germantown beat Starkville by 7, Starkville beat Taylorsville by 4, and Taylorsville beat Germantown by 1, Germantown would have 6 points, Starkville would have -3 points, and Taylorsville would have -3 points. This means Germantown would be first, and Taylorsville and Starkville would revert back to the first step (head to head competition)
5.	Point differential (maximum of 12) allowed in the region games involving the highest ranked or seeded teams.
6.	Fewest points allowed in all region games – (No Maximum Points)
7.	The two schools flip a coin
